# Overview

The proposal's information falls into two categories.

Product Opportunity Brief
=========================

- **Investment Recommendation** — Decides the characteristics of the final portfolio, such as expected return and risk profile.
- **Supporting Analysis** — Derived from the Investment Recommendation to help the investor more easily understand the rationale and risk profile. This includes the Executive Summary and scenario analysis.

# Investment Recommendation

- Suggested product and position
- Funding source / reallocation plan
- Expected return and risk profile change (vs. current portfolio)
- Bullet-point pros and cons from a portfolio perspective
- Alternative suggested products

# Supporting Analysis

- Executive summary
- Product specification
- Pie chart of asset allocation
- Portfolio constituents table
- Scenario analysis
- Risk disclaimer

---

## Proposed Table of Contents

1. **Executive Summary** — One-page overview of the recommendation, key rationale, and expected outcomes.

2. **Suggested Product & Position** — Which product is recommended, target allocation percentage, and rationale for selection.

3. **Funding Source / Reallocation Plan** — Where the capital comes from (cash, redemption, rebalancing) and any sell/buy trade instructions.

4. **Expected Return & Risk Profile Change** — Projected return, volatility, concentration risk, and how the portfolio's risk-return profile shifts versus the current portfolio.

5. **Pros & Cons from a Portfolio Perspective** — Bullet-point summary of how the recommendation strengthens or weakens the portfolio.

6. **Alternative Products Considered** — Other products evaluated and why the recommended product was chosen over them.

7. **Product Specification** — Key facts: issuer, asset class, fees, liquidity, maturity, and any structural features.

8. **Asset Allocation Pie Chart** — Visual showing the proposed portfolio composition.

9. **Portfolio Constituents Table** — Detailed breakdown of all holdings after the recommended trade.

10. **Scenario Analysis** — How the portfolio performs under adverse and favourable market conditions.

11. **Risk Disclaimer** — Standard disclosures and limitations of the analysis.
