A typical investment proposal has the following sections

- Suggested product & position
- Funding for the product 
- Expect return and risk profile (e.g., concentration risk) difference
- Point form on pros and cons from portfolio perspective
- Alternative suggested products

- Executive summary
- Product spec
- Pie chart on asset allocation
- Table on the portfolio constituent
- Scenario analysis
- Risk disclaimer
